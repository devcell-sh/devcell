## Tier Notes

### Tier 1 (reach, but harder to get)

- **Fireship** — 4.1M YouTube. Covers new dev tools regularly. A Fireship video would be massive. Approach: cold DM + a
  sharp 1-liner about the problem ("Fireship-worthy" pitch: "the tool that finally makes --dangerously-skip-permissions
  safe"). Very selective, but public DMs open.
- **Theo** — 243k Twitter. Opinionated tech takes. Would need a compelling angle; could work as a Twitter thread or a "
  hot take" post. Contact: @theo on X.

### Tier 2 (best fit, reachable)

- **Nate's Newsletter** — Already writes detailed Claude Code guides for wannabes. Perfect fit. Likely to review devcell
  if pitched as solving the auto-approve fear. Substack DM or reply to existing post.
- **Jack Herrington** — Practical fullstack focus, mid-tier audience that actually builds things. Contact via X @jherr
  or email on site.

### Tier 3 (long tail, seed organic mentions)

- CG Strategy Lab, codewithmukesh, Nathan Onn — Smaller reach but highly relevant to the Claude Code vibe coder
  audience. Could be seeded via "can you review this tool?" DM or providing free access.

## Discovery Channels for More Influencers

- Search YouTube: `"claude code" setup 2026` — filter for 10k–200k subscriber channels with recent uploads
- Search Twitter/X: `"claude code" auto-approve` — find accounts with high engagement who post about this
- Search Reddit: r/vibecoding mod list, top posters — community leaders who may have Twitter/YouTube presence
- Product Hunt launch: AI tool reviewers who upvote developer tools in the "dev tools" category often have newsletters

## Notes for Outreach

- **Do NOT lead with "Docker container"** — use "sandbox" or "safe auto-approve" as the hook
- **Best hook for technical influencers:** "What happens when you run --dangerously-skip-permissions? We made a sandbox
  so you don't have to find out the hard way."
- **Best hook for wannabe-oriented creators:** "I was terrified to let Claude run freely. Here's what I use now."
- Offer a guest post / co-created demo video for Tier 2 targets
- For Tier 1: Send a one-liner pitch and a demo GIF. No long email.
